LEO is an AI company based in Delhi, India. We work on modern technology and artificial intelligence. Our aim is to make useful AI products for people.

We believe that good technology should be simple, useful and easy to understand.

Hiring Process

The hiring process is simple and has different stages.

Hiring Terms

Screening
Checking the candidate's application and resume.

HR Round
An interview about communication and personality.
Offer
The final job offer given to a selected candidate.
