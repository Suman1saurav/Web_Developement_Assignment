GLA University Students Participate in Technology Event

Student Name: Suman Saurav
College Name: GLA University

GLA University organized a technology-focused event for students. The event provided students with an opportunity to demonstrate their technical knowledge, creativity, and problem-solving abilities.

The event was held on August 27, 2026 at GLA University. More than 500 students participated in the event. Students presented projects related to Computer Science, Networking, Artificial Intelligence, and other modern technologies.

Students actively participated in different activities and learned about new technologies. The event encouraged students to develop practical skills and think creatively.

Suman Saurav, a first-year BTech Computer Science student at GLA University, also showed interest in the technology activities. Such events help students gain practical knowledge and improve their communication and teamwork skills.

Editor's Note

Technology events provide students with valuable opportunities to learn beyond the classroom. They help students develop confidence, technical skills, teamwork, and practical knowledge for their future careers.
