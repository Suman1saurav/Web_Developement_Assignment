Student Terms and Rules

General Rules

Students are expected to maintain a responsible and respectful attitude throughout their academic life.

Students must carry their valid college ID card on campus.
Students must follow all college rules and instructions issued by faculty or administration.
Students should maintain cleanliness in classrooms, laboratories, libraries, and other common areas.
These rules help create a safe, organized, and productive learning environment.

Example: A student entering the campus for a laboratory session may be asked to show their ID card at the entrance.

Attendance Rules

Regular attendance is essential for academic progress.

Students must maintain the minimum attendance percentage required by the college.
Students must attend lectures, practicals, tutorials, and other compulsory academic activities.
Students should inform the concerned faculty member in advance if they are unable to attend a class.
Attendance requirements may differ depending on the course or university regulations.

Penalty
Students falling below the required attendance may be prohibited from appearing in examinations or may face other academic penalties.

Example:If a course requires 75% attendance, a student attending only 60% of classes may not be eligible to sit for the final examination.

Examination Rules

Examinations must be conducted fairly and honestly.

Students must bring their valid ID card and required examination materials.
Mobile phones, smartwatches, or unauthorized electronic devices must not be used during examinations.
Students must not communicate with or copy from another student.
Students should arrive at the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the scheduled start time.

Academic Misconduct
Cheating, copying, impersonation, or using unauthorized materials may result in cancellation of the examination and disciplinary action.

The exact penalty depends on the seriousness of the violation and institutional policy.

Example: A student found using notes hidden inside their examination desk may have their paper cancelled.

Assignment Submission

Assignments must be completed according to the instructions provided by the faculty.

Example Deadline

Submission Deadline: 15 September 2026, 11:59 PM

Assignment Requirements:

Minimum 1,500 words
Font: Times New Roman, 12 pt
Heading: 16 pt, bold
Line spacing: 1.5
Include Student name, Roll number, Course, and Submission date
Add references/sources where required.
Submit the file in PDF format unless another format is specified.

Submission Instructions

Complete the assignment according to the given requirements.
Save the file using the format: Name_RollNo_Subject.pdf
Upload it to the designated college portal.
Check that the uploaded file opens correctly.
Submit before the deadline.

Submission Penalty

Up to 24 hours late - 10% deduction

24–48 hours late - 20% deduction

More than 48 hours late - Submission may not be accepted.

Late submissions may be considered only in exceptional circumstances with prior approval.

Example: If an assignment is worth 100 marks and is submitted 12 hours late, the student may receive a maximum of 90 marks after the 10% penalty.

Discipline & Behaviour

Students must behave respectfully toward faculty, staff, and fellow students.

Bullying, harassment, threats, or abusive behaviour are strictly prohibited.
Students must not damage college property.
Students should maintain appropriate behaviour in classrooms and public areas.
Students must follow laboratory and safety instructions.
Disagreements should be resolved respectfully through the appropriate faculty or administrative channel.

Penalties
Violations may result in:
Warning → Written notice → Fine → Suspension → Further disciplinary action
The penalty depends on the nature and seriousness of the violation.

Technology & Internet Usage

College technology and internet facilities should be used responsibly.

Students must not access, distribute, or upload illegal or harmful content using college systems.
Students must not attempt to access another person's account without permission.
Students should protect their passwords and account information.
College computers should be used primarily for academic purposes.

Example: Using a college computer to complete a programming assignment is permitted, while attempting to access another student's account is prohibited.

Campus & Property Rules

Students are responsible for taking care of college facilities.

Students must not intentionally damage furniture, laboratory equipment, computers, or other college property.
Students should dispose of waste in designated bins.
Unauthorized removal of college equipment or materials is prohibited.
Students must follow safety instructions in laboratories and workshops.

Damage Penalty

Students responsible for intentional damage may be required to pay the repair or replacement cost and may face disciplinary action.

Example: If a student intentionally damages laboratory equipment, the college may require the student to compensate for the repair or replacement.

Important Reminder

Students are responsible for knowing and following all applicable college rules, academic regulations, deadlines, and instructions issued by the institution.

Always check the official notice, course portal, or faculty instructions for the latest deadlines and requirements.

Rules and penalties may vary between institutions, courses, and academic sessions.
