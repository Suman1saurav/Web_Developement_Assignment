Explore Jaipur, Rajasthan

Welcome to Jaipur, a vibrant destination known for its magnificent forts, palaces, handicrafts and traditional cuisine. Whether you enjoy history, photography, shopping or local food, Jaipur offers experiences for different kinds of travellers.

From the hilltop grandeur of Amber Fort to the detailed architecture of the City Palace, visitors can experience centuries of culture in a single trip.

Recommended season: October–March for comfortable sightseeing.

Travel Tips

Before You Travel

  • Carry valid photo identification and copies of important documents.
  • Wear comfortable footwear for heritage sites.
  • Carry drinking water and protect yourself from strong sunlight.
  • Check attraction opening hours before leaving your accommodation.
  • Use licensed guides and authorised transport providers where possible.
  • Keep valuables secure in crowded markets and tourist areas.

One-Day Jaipur Itinerary

  1. 8:00 AM: Start with Amber Fort.
  2. 11:00 AM: Visit Jal Mahal viewpoint and continue toward the old city.
  3. 12:00 PM: Explore City Palace and Jantar Mantar.
  4. 2:30 PM: Take a lunch break and try local cuisine.
  5. 4:00 PM: Visit Hawa Mahal and explore nearby markets.
  6. 6:00 PM: Finish with an evening viewpoint or cultural experience.
