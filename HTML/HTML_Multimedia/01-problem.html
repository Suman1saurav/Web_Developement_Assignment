Welcome to North Valley University

North Valley University (NVU) is a student-focused institution offering career-oriented education, research opportunities, and an active campus experience. Our programs are designed to help students develop knowledge, practical skills, and confidence.

Explore our programs, check your eligibility, learn about scholarships, and submit your application online. Admission requirements may vary by program, so applicants should read the official information carefully.

Admissions

Admission Announcement

2027 Admissions are open! Eligible students can submit applications for selected programs before the published deadline. Shortlisted applicants may be invited for an entrance test, interview, counselling, or document verification depending on the program.

Applications received after the deadline may not be considered. Applicants should keep original academic documents available for verification.

Proceed to the Apply Now form | Download Admission Brochure

Application Guidance

Before applying:

  1. Review the program eligibility requirements.
  2. Choose the program that matches your qualification.
  3. Prepare academic and personal information.
  4. Complete the online application form accurately.
  5. Review all information before submitting the application.
  6. Save your application reference number for future communication.

Tip: Use a working email address and mobile number because important admission updates may be sent through these channels.

Important Admission Information

Eligibility
Applicants must satisfy the academic and subject requirements specified for their selected program.
Scholarship
Merit-based and need-based scholarships may be available to eligible students, subject to university rules.
Merit List
Shortlisting may be based on academic performance, entrance results, interview performance, or other published criteria.
Application Fee
The application processing fee, where applicable, must be paid according to the instructions in the official admission notice.
